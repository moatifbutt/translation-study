Perceptual evaluation of image-to-image translation

Unpaired image-to-image translation methods map an image from a source domain to a target domain while preserving its underlying structure. This study evaluates such methods on three domain pairs (cat and dog, dog and wild animal, male and female faces) in both directions. In each trial you are shown a source image together with two translations produced by different methods. Please select the translation that is more realistic as an image of the target domain and that better preserves the pose, composition and background of the source image.
